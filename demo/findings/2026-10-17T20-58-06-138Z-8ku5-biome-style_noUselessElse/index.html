Rule analysis

Flags `else` blocks following an `if` whose body contains a breaking statement (`return`, `break`, `continue`, or `throw`).

Fix: Removes the `else` keywords and braces, promoting the `else` block's statements to the same indentation level as the enclosing `if` statement.

10 hazards
  • Moving a `var`-declared variable from the `else` block to the outer scope may cause it to be hoisted and accessed before initialization if referenced earlier in the function.
  • If the `else` block contains a `let` or `const` declaration that shadows an outer variable, promoting it may alter which binding inner functions capture due to changed scope nesting.
  • Promoting statements from the `else` block may reorder their execution relative to other statements in the same block if the original `if-else` was not the first or last statement.
  • If the `else` block contains a `break` targeting an outer loop, moving it outside the `if` may cause it to execute unconditionally, altering loop termination.
  • A `continue` statement promoted from the `else` block may apply to a different loop than intended if loop nesting changes due to the removal of the `else` block's braces.
  • A `throw` statement moved from the `else` block may be caught by a different `try`/`catch` if the `if-else` was inside a `try` block and the promotion changes the nesting level.
  • Comments attached to the `else` keyword or its braces may be lost or misplaced during the rewrite, as the autofix typically does not preserve syntactic comments on removed tokens.
  • If the `else` block is the sole content of a `case` clause in a `switch`, removing its braces may fall through to the next case if the promoted statements do not end with a `break`.
  • In a `with` block, promoting `else` statements may change the object used for property resolution if the `if` and `else` blocks previously shared the same `with` context but the promotion alters evaluation order.
  • If the `if` statement lacks braces and the autofix incorrectly assumes block structure, it may corrupt syntax by inserting or deleting braces where none existed.
