Rule analysis

Flags redundant 'continue' statements at the end of loops where they have no effect.

Fix: Removes the redundant 'continue;' statement entirely from the loop body.

8 hazards
  • Removing a labeled 'continue' may alter control flow if outer loops depend on skipping iterations via the label.
  • If the 'continue' is the only statement in a loop body, removing it creates an empty loop body which may be invalid or change semantics in edge cases involving ASI.
  • In a 'do-while' loop, removing the 'continue' may cause the condition to be evaluated on the first iteration when it previously was not, altering side-effect timing.
  • If the loop contains no other statements and the 'continue' is removed, some minifiers or tools might treat the empty block differently, risking parsing issues in strict contexts.
  • Removing a 'continue' that follows a side-effecting expression (e.g., after a function call) could change evaluation order if the autofix incorrectly assumes the continue is last when it isn't due to formatting.
  • In loops with multiple labels, removing a labeled 'continue' might break intended jumps to outer loops if inner loops rely on the label for control flow.
  • If the 'continue' is followed by a comment that relies on its presence for JSDoc or tooling interpretation, removing it may detach or misalign the comment.
  • In a loop with a trailing semicolon after the block (rare), removing the 'continue' could expose the semicolon as an empty statement, potentially confusing parsers or linters.
