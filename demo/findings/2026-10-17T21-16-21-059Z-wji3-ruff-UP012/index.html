Rule analysis

Flags redundant `.encode("utf-8")` or `.encode(encoding="utf-8")` calls where UTF-8 is the default.

Fix: Replace `.encode("utf-8")` with `b"..."` for ASCII-only string literals, otherwise replace with `.encode()` (dropping the encoding argument).
