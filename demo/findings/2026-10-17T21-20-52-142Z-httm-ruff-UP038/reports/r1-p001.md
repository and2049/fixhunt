## Why the rewrite is not equivalent (model draft, verify before use)

The original class defines `__bool__`, making instances evaluate to `True`. Removing this method causes truthiness to fall back to string length—empty strings become falsy, non-empty remain true—but since `__str__` inherits from `str`, any instance’s value determines its boolean behavior, unlike the hardcoded `True` in `__bool__`. Thus, rewriting by deleting `__bool__` alters semantics for empty or non-empty string values, breaking equivalence.
